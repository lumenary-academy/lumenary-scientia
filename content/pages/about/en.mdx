## What Lumenary Scientia covers

Lumenary Scientia exists to make rigorous, joyful and intellectually liberating education in the sciences available to people everywhere — for free, in their own languages, without accounts, third-party ads or paid subscriptions.

- **[Lumenary Math](../math/)**, its first subject, runs from counting in kindergarten to the first year of graduate school, with optional pathways into research: 201 lessons and 19 Wonder Trails.
- **[Lumenary Statistics](/en/statistics/)** runs from averages and charts to Bayesian computation and causal inference.
- **[Lumenary CS](/en/cs/)** runs from a first program to the frontier of AI, with courses in the world's major [programming languages](/en/cs/languages/) and [electives](/en/cs/electives/).
- **[Lumenary EE](/en/ee/)**, **[Lumenary Robotics](/en/robotics/)**, **[Lumenary Chemistry](/en/chemistry/)**, **[Lumenary ME](/en/me/)** and **[Lumenary Materials](/en/materials/)** each run from first steps to the frontier of 2026.
- Physics, biology and more follow, built the same way and under the same licences.

Lumenary Scientia is one of the three divisions of Lumenary Academy, with [Lumenary Lingua](https://lingua.lumenary.academy) for languages and [Lumenary Prep](https://prep.lumenary.academy) for exam preparation; Lumenary Academy's [About page](https://lumenary.academy/en/about/) describes the academy as a whole, and its [divisions page](https://lumenary.academy/en/divisions/) the three divisions.

> **All the sciences. Many languages. Every nation.**

## How lessons are drafted and checked

Every lesson was drafted with the help of AI systems. AI is a collaborator, not an oracle: what a machine can check was checked by machine, and everything else awaits human review.

- **Lean.** Lumenary Math's formal proofs are written in Lean 4 with Mathlib and compiled by continuous integration on every change. A lesson is labelled **formally verified** only when every Lean declaration it cites appears in the latest build report, compiled, and does not depend on `sorry`. Each proof lists the axioms it uses, and we distinguish proofs that apply an existing library theorem from those proved here.
- **Computed experiments.** Lumenary Math's Python experiments run in your browser, and the same files are run and tested by automated tests. An experiment is labelled **computationally tested** only when it ran and its automated tests passed, with an honest label for what kind of evidence a computation provides.
- **Code checks.** The code in the other subjects' lessons is run by automated checks: the Python on every change (it also runs in your browser), and the programs in other languages, compiled and run with each language's own toolchain (Verilog with Icarus Verilog, circuits with ngspice), whenever the code report is regenerated.

The [verification report](../verification/) shows exactly what has been checked, by what, and what has not.

## How we teach

Every suitable idea is explored in three complementary modes:

- **Human** — intuition, examples, pictures, conjectures and clear human proofs.
- **Formal** — precise definitions and machine-checked proofs in Lean 4.
- **Computational** — Python experiments that run in your browser, with honest labels for what kind of evidence a computation provides.

The modes reinforce one another rather than living in separate books. Three cat guides — **Chloe**, **Margaret** and **Gideon** — appear in lessons at every level. In Lumenary Math they are the Mathematician, the Computationalist and the Formalist, asking the questions each mode cares about; in the other subjects each takes that subject's roles, such as the Chemist, the Analyst and the Sceptic in Lumenary Chemistry. If you prefer to read without them, the cat button at the top of every page hides them.

Our guiding principle is: **Master the present. Glimpse the future. Never require the future to understand the present.** Optional *Wonder Trails* invite curious learners to glimpse where an idea leads — towards groups, quotients, infinity or category theory — without ever counting against anyone who doesn't follow them.

## Foundations: sets and categories

Earlier mathematics is built on **sets**. Higher mathematics increasingly speaks the structural language of **categories**. Lumenary Math teaches both, from intuitive precursors in primary school to their rigorous forms at university — and it is careful about how they relate, without pretending they are interchangeable.

## Honesty

We are still at the beginning. Every lesson was drafted with AI assistance and none has yet been reviewed by a human expert in its field. We would rather say so plainly than claim a trustworthiness we have not yet earned; expert review is the most valuable help anyone can give.

Lumenary Scientia aims to be more trustworthy than ordinary AI-generated material:

- A label such as **formally verified** or **computationally tested** is derived from the latest build and test reports, never asserted by hand.
- **No content has yet been reviewed by a human mathematician, scientist, educator or translator.** Every lesson says so. Agreement between AI systems is not treated as evidence of correctness.
- Translations other than English are machine-generated drafts and are labelled as such.

We will teach learners to use AI the same way — to ask good questions, critique arguments, find counterexamples and verify claims, rather than to depend on it.

## Christian inspiration — offered, never imposed

Lumenary Scientia, like the rest of Lumenary Academy, is inspired by historic Christian values. We say so openly, because transparency is one of them. These values shape *how* we build and *how* we treat learners:

- **Truthfulness** — we never claim a proof, test, review or translation that has not happened.
- **Wisdom and humility** — we label uncertainty, admit our limitations, and welcome correction.
- **Love of neighbour and human dignity** — every learner, everywhere, deserves patient, respectful teaching. We never use shame, manipulative streaks or public rankings.
- **Generosity** — everything is free and openly licensed.
- **Stewardship** — the site is light, works on inexpensive devices, and is cheap to host.
- **Intellectual honesty** — mathematical statements are justified mathematically, never by authority.
- **Wonder at creation** — mathematics and the sciences are beautiful, and curiosity is worth cultivating for its own sake.
- **Service** — the project exists for its learners, not the other way round.

Christian inspiration is **never a prerequisite**. You do not need to share any belief to learn here, and nothing in the mathematics or the sciences depends on one. A few lessons contain short, clearly marked *optional reflections*; they are collapsed by default, separate from the instruction, and can simply be skipped.

We represent mathematicians, scientists and their traditions of every religious and non-religious background accurately and respectfully. **Truth is not determined by the identity of the person who discovers it.**
